    How it works

    1. 01

      The clock. It keeps running and hands your answers in at 00:00. With the team in, it pauses while each answer is on show.

    2. 02

      One go each. An answer is locked in once you submit. Skipping counts as wrong.

    3. 03

      Typed answers. A word or two is plenty. Capitals and accents don't matter.

    4. 04

      Points. Right answers earn points. With the team in, what they get right or wrong moves your points too (see How points work).

    5. 05

      Critical questions. Flagged Critical, they count double.

    6. 06

      Shortcuts. A–F to choose, Enter to submit.
