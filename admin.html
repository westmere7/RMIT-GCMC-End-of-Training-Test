Settings

People

Sign-in accepts any email and any staff ID (testing build, no real authentication). Anyone without a saved name is asked for their first name when they sign in, and it's saved here. Changes to people save straight away. Emails are stored as SHA-256 fingerprints.

Questions

Written answers ignore capitals, accents, spaces and punctuation, so “Thursday.”, “thursday” and “THURSDAY” all match. For fill-in questions, put ___ (three underscores) where the blank goes.
